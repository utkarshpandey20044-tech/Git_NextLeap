# Sample Q&A

## 1. Minimum SIP --- SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund

**Question:** What is the minimum SIP amount for SBI ELSS Tax Saver
Fund?

**Answer:** The minimum amount for SIP for SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund is
₹500 and investments can be made in multiples of ₹500.

**Source:**
https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-elss-tax-saver-fund-factsheet-march-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=29d79745_2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Benchmark --- SBI Multicap Fund

**Question:** What is the benchmark of SBI Multicap Fund?

**Answer:** The benchmark index of SBI Multicap Fund is the NIFTY 500
Multicap 50:25:25 TRI Index.

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/campaign/sbi-multicap-fund

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Exit load --- SBI Flexicap Fund

**Question:** What is the exit load of SBI Flexicap Fund?

**Answer:** The exit load is 0.10% for exits on or before 30 days from
the date of allotment and Nil for exits after 30 days.

**Source:**
https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-flexicap-fund-factsheet-march-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=39a6ea88_2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Account statement

**Question:** How do I download my account statement?

**Answer:** On the SBI Mutual Fund website, select "I am an Existing
Investor" and use "Get Statements"; available statements include Account
Statement, Capital Gains Statement, and Smart Statement.

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/ways-to-invest

------------------------------------------------------------------------
